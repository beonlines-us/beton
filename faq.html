FAQ

Everything you might want to know about the EpicRush Trivia Quiz.

Is the quiz suitable for all ages?

Absolutely! We focus on creating a fun, general knowledge quiz that can be enjoyed by everyone.

Does it work on my tablet?

Yes! Our quiz is designed to work perfectly on tablets, phones, and computers using any modern browser.

Do I need to download anything?

Nope! Our quiz plays right in your web browser. No installs or app store visits needed.

Are there any hidden costs?

Our quiz is free to play! We want to make sure everyone can enjoy the fun without worry.

How are high scores saved?

Your scores are saved right on your own device, so you can see your progress next time you visit.

Can I contact you?

Yes! We welcome feedback from players. You can reach out through our contact page or email us directly.

How do I play the quiz?

Simply read the question and select the answer you think is correct. It's that easy!

Can I play with my friends?

While our current quiz is single-player focused, you can challenge your friends to beat your high score!

Does the quiz help me learn?

Our quiz focuses on general knowledge, helping you learn new facts and test your memory in a fun way.

How often are new questions added?

We are always working on new questions and hope to add more fun categories frequently!

Is my data private?

We take privacy seriously. We don't collect unnecessary personal information from our players.

What if I find an error?

If you find an error in a question or something isn't working right, please let us know via the contact form so we can fix it!
